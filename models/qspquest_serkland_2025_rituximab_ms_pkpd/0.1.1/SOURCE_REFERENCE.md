# Rituximab exposure–CD19+ B-cell dynamics

Coupled PK/PD model of circulating rituximab and CD19+ B-cell dynamics in multiple sclerosis. Rituximab declines through linear and B-cell-dependent loss, drives CD19+ cell depletion, and is linked to gradual B-cell repopulation through baseline production and turnover.

Source scientific documents and source terms are included in this OMEX archive. Ubi contributions are Apache-2.0; attributed source content retains CC BY 4.0.
