# Choi 2025 Denosumab SB16 Biosimilarity PopPK/PD

Fixed-effect ODE reconstruction of the Choi et al. 2025 denosumab SB16 PopPK/PD model: SC first-order absorption, two-compartment QSS target-mediated disposition against RANKL, and indirect-response lumbar spine BMD turnover. Curated scenarios are typical-subject projections. Population distributions, source-figure reproduction and fitting remain incomplete; historical scalar replays are disabled reference records.

Source scientific documents and source terms are included in this OMEX archive. Ubi contributions are Apache-2.0; attributed source content retains CC BY 4.0.

11 of 11 source presets are independently matched with the current engine and exposed for standalone simulation. Others remain source-only with explicit holds. This is not population-fitting or clinical qualification.
